    This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app. "We" refers to the app's developer (the person shown as the app's seller on the App Store).

    In short

    • The app has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

    • To improve the app, we only look at usage statistics and error reports that are not linked to your identity.

    What stays on your device

    Your preferences, progress, likes, downloaded content and anything you write in the app (such as your prayers) are stored on your device. Unless a feature clearly says otherwise, they are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them too.

    Usage statistics and error reports

    To understand how the app is used and to fix errors, usage events (such as which screens are opened and which features are used) and error reports are collected. Along with them, your device model, operating system, app version, language, a random app identifier and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city) are processed. This information is not linked to your name or identity.

    Purchases

    Subscriptions are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don't see your payment details. To check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases, your subscription status and App Store purchase history are processed with a random identifier.

    Shared counts and content from the internet

    Some features show counts that everyone sees; your contribution is added only as a number, without being linked to your identity. When content such as audio is loaded from the internet, your IP address is processed, as with any internet request.

    When you write to us

    When you email us, your email address and message are used to reply to you. Error reports may include technical details such as the app version and device model; you can see them before sending.

    Service providers

    For these purposes we work with service providers for analytics, error reporting, subscription management and content delivery. Their servers may be located outside Türkiye, so information may be transferred to other countries in line with applicable data protection law.

    Purpose, legal basis and retention

    This information is processed to provide the app, manage subscriptions, improve the app and fix errors. It is based on providing the app to you, our legitimate interest in keeping the app working well and securely and, where required, legal obligations. It is kept only as long as these purposes need, and is then deleted or anonymised.

    Children

    The app is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

    Your rights

    Depending on where you live (for example under KVKK in Türkiye or the GDPR in the European Union), you may have rights such as requesting information, asking for deletion or objecting to processing. You can write to us to exercise them.

    Because the app has no accounts and information is not linked to your identity, we may ask for additional details to fulfil your request. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets the random identifiers.

    Changes

    We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is published on this page; the date at the top shows the latest change.

    मक़सद, क़ानूनी आधार और रखने की अवधि

    यह जानकारी ऐप उपलब्ध कराने, सब्सक्रिप्शन संभालने, ऐप को बेहतर बनाने और ग़लतियाँ ठीक करने के लिए प्रोसेस की जाती है। इसका आधार है: आपको ऐप उपलब्ध कराना, ऐप के ठीक और सुरक्षित ढंग से चलने में हमारा जायज़ हित, और जहाँ ज़रूरी हो, क़ानूनी ज़िम्मेदारियाँ। यह जानकारी सिर्फ़ उतने समय तक रखी जाती है जितना इन मक़सदों के लिए ज़रूरी हो; उसके बाद इसे डिलीट कर दिया जाता है या गुमनाम बना दिया जाता है।

    बच्चे

    ऐप ख़ास तौर पर बच्चों के लिए नहीं बनाया गया है। हम जान-बूझकर 13 साल से कम उम्र के बच्चों से निजी जानकारी जमा नहीं करते।

    جب آپ ہمیں ای میل کرتے ہیں تو آپ کا ای میل پتا اور پیغام آپ کو جواب دینے کے لیے استعمال ہوتا ہے۔ خرابی کی اطلاع میں ایپ کا ورژن اور ڈیوائس کا ماڈل جیسی تکنیکی تفصیلات شامل ہو سکتی ہیں؛ بھیجنے سے پہلے آپ انہیں دیکھ سکتے ہیں۔

    سروس فراہم کنندگان

    ان مقاصد کے لیے ہم تجزیات، خرابی کی رپورٹنگ، سبسکرپشن کے انتظام اور مواد کی ترسیل کے شعبوں میں سروس فراہم کنندگان کے ساتھ کام کرتے ہیں۔ ان کے سرور ترکی سے باہر ہو سکتے ہیں، اس لیے معلومات ڈیٹا کے تحفظ کے قابلِ اطلاق قوانین کے مطابق دوسرے ممالک کو منتقل کی جا سکتی ہیں۔

    مقصد، قانونی بنیاد اور رکھنے کی مدت

    یہ معلومات ایپ فراہم کرنے، سبسکرپشن کا انتظام کرنے، ایپ کو بہتر بنانے اور خرابیاں دور کرنے کے لیے پراسیس کی جاتی ہیں۔ اس پراسیسنگ کی بنیاد آپ کو ایپ فراہم کرنا، ایپ کے ٹھیک اور محفوظ طریقے سے کام کرنے میں ہمارا جائز مفاد، اور جہاں ضروری ہو، قانونی ذمہ داریاں ہیں۔ یہ معلومات صرف اتنی مدت تک رکھی جاتی ہیں جتنی ان مقاصد کے لیے ضروری ہو؛ اس کے بعد انہیں حذف یا گمنام کر دیا جاتا ہے۔
